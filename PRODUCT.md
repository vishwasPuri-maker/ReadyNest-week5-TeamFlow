# Product

## Register

product

> The application (dashboard, tasks, members, analytics) is the primary surface and is a **product** register. The marketing landing page at `/` is a **brand** surface and is designed under the brand register.

## Users

Small-to-mid software and product teams (5–50 people) who juggle work across multiple clients or internal squads. Each organization is an isolated tenant. Two roles matter: **Admins** who set up the workspace, invite people, and own structure; and **Members** who live in the task board day to day. On the landing page, the visitor is a team lead or founder evaluating whether TeamFlow is credible and fast enough to adopt.

## Product Purpose

TeamFlow is a multi-tenant SaaS for project & task management: organizations, role-based access, real-time task updates, dashboards/analytics, file attachments, audit logs, and full auth (JWT + email verification + password reset). Success = a visitor understands, in one scroll, exactly what the product does and trusts that it's real, secure, and production-grade — then signs up.

## Brand Personality

Precise, confident, engineered. Voice is plain and direct — no hype adjectives, no "revolutionary". It should feel like a tool built by people who ship: calm surfaces, exact spacing, real product shown honestly. Three words: **precise · fast · trustworthy.**

## Anti-references

- Warm-cream / beige "SaaS template" landing pages with a soft gradient blob hero — the saturated AI-generated look.
- Repeated tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section.
- Identical 3-up feature card grids with a rounded icon + heading + lorem.
- Gradient text, glassmorphism-by-default, purple-blob backgrounds.
- Fake customer logos or invented testimonials/metrics.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the real product.** Imagery is honest, high-fidelity product UI (dashboard, task board), not abstract illustration.
2. **Scroll narrates capability.** Each fold explains one real feature with its matching visual — the page teaches what the app does.
3. **Swiss precision.** Strict grid, true off-white (never cream), black ink, one saturated accent, big grotesque headlines, generous whitespace.
4. **Motion with intent.** Entrance choreography and scroll reveals that enhance already-visible content; every animation has a reduced-motion fallback.
5. **Earn trust with specifics.** Name the actual capabilities (multi-tenant isolation, real-time, RBAC, audit logs) instead of vague promises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. Full keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic landmarks. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` with crossfade/instant fallbacks. Content is visible by default — reveals never gate visibility.
